# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Dota 2 streamers and content creators who produce multi-hour VODs and want to split them automatically into individual match files. Used in a post-stream workflow — they just finished a session and want match footage ready for editing or archiving without manual scrubbing.

## Product Purpose

Automates finding match boundaries in Dota 2 VODs via computer vision (HUD detection). User pastes a URL, the system downloads, analyzes, and outputs individual match MP4s for download. Success = less time between streaming and having isolated match files.

## Brand Personality

Precise, fast, no-nonsense. Respects that creators are busy.

Three words: efficient, creator-native, focused

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS hero pages: gradient backgrounds, big metric numbers, identical feature card grids
- Over-gamified esports aesthetics: particle effects, glowing animated borders, excessive neon
- Enterprise gray-blue corporate tools

## Design Principles

1. **The pipeline is the UI.** Progress feedback is the core value — every state must be visible and readable at a glance.
2. **Tool disappears into the task.** Minimal chrome, maximum clarity where it counts. Job status dominates, not brand.
3. **State over decoration.** Every visual element earns its place by communicating job state. No decoration that doesn't carry information.
4. **Creator-environment defaults.** Dark-by-system — editing, streaming, and monitoring happen on dark setups. Match the environment.
5. **Passive feedback matters most.** Creators submit and walk away. Polling and progress indicators are the product's core interaction.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
